The Virginia Reels

Brussels-based country music trio

The Virginia Reels play classic country and western music by legends like Johnny Cash and Loretta Lynn, blending rich harmonies with fiddle, banjo, electric guitar and accordion. Expect a laid-back show that draws on our Texan and Virginian roots to deliver songs of love and heartbreak.

If you'd like us to play at your venue or event, please email band@thevirginiareels.com
